Our work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, computer vision and medical imaging**, with a focus on deep probabilistic and variational models that quantify uncertainty and generalise across populations.

## 🫀 Cardiac motion and shape analysis
Learning how the heart moves and deforms from cardiac MRI: motion estimation, joint segmentation and registration, realistic sequence generation, and unsupervised detection of shape abnormalities (e.g. *CardioMorphNet*, *SegMorph*, *DragNet*).

## 🔬 Cancer imaging and computational pathology
Multiple-instance and vision-language models for whole-slide histopathology, including immune subtyping of melanoma and slide-level reasoning beyond individual patches.

## ☢️ Radiotherapy outcome prediction
Combining imaging, dose distributions and clinical data to predict treatment toxicity after pelvic radiotherapy, and learning-based motion estimation for image-guided treatment (e.g. *4D-Precise*, *Deep-Motion-Net*).

## 📐 Computational anatomy and population inference
Generative models for shape matching and generation, group-wise analysis of imaging data, and statistical inference on large imaging populations such as UK Biobank.
