## 🧠 Gooya Lab

We develop **deep probabilistic and variational learning** methods that turn medical images into reliable, interpretable insight for clinicians.

The lab is based in the **School of Computing Science, University of Glasgow**, and is led by Dr Ali Gooya.

**Research interests:**

- Cardiac image analysis and motion modelling
- Cancer imaging, histopathology and radiotherapy outcome prediction
- Computer-aided decision support
- Prediction and marker discovery
- Statistical inference on populations and computational anatomy
