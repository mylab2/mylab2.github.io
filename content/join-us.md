We welcome enquiries from motivated students and researchers interested in **machine learning for medical imaging**.

## PhD students
Applicants with a strong background in computer science, engineering, mathematics or physics are encouraged to get in touch. Please include your CV, transcripts and a short statement of research interests aligned with our [research themes](/research/).

Funding information: [University of Glasgow scholarships](https://www.gla.ac.uk/scholarships/).

## Visiting researchers and students
We host visiting students and researchers. Please email a short proposal and your CV.
